Semua memakai pola yang sama: fitur dari sel 12 `notebook/v10.ipynb` (`build_all` = `eda/features.py`), 5 outer fold
`splits(y, 5, 42)`, early stopping LightGBM di inner split 1/7 train fold (`splits(y[tr], 7, 300+fold)`, **bersih**),
lr .02, leaves 31, min_child 100, ff .5, bagging .8, l2 5. Builder `notebook/build_vNN.py`, kode
`notebook/vNN_experiment.py`, hasil `results/vNN/vNN/` (oof.parquet, test.parquet, metrics.json, log.csv).
